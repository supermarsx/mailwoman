# Mailwoman manual

Mail, your way. Find the settings and workflows you need, from your first mailbox to running a full deployment.

Use the **topic sidebar** to browse, or **Search** (Ctrl K / ⌘ K) to find a feature across the manual. Search runs locally in your browser. Use the theme switch in the header to choose a light or dark appearance; the manual starts in dark mode.

## Find your way

The sidebar groups the complete manual by topic and highlights the page you are reading. On smaller screens, choose **Menu** to open it. Each article has an **On this page** outline and previous/next navigation. Code samples, search results, tables and callouts follow the selected theme.
