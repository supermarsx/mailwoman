# The mailbox

The mailbox is the main screen of Mailwoman. It shows the folders of your account, the messages in the selected folder, and the message you have open. This page names the parts of the screen. The pages that follow explain what you do in each part.

![The mailbox with the folder sidebar on the left, the message list in the middle and an opened message on the right, with markers 1 to 6 on the sidebar, the tabs, the toolbar, the search box, the message list and the reader.](/screenshots/user-mailbox/mailbox-tour.webp){.mw-shot}

1. The folder sidebar. The marker sits beside **Inbox**.
2. The tab strip, here with the **Mail** tab.
3. The toolbar above the message list. The marker sits on its last button, **Check for new mail**.
4. The search box.
5. The message list.
6. The reader, which shows the open message.
{.mw-callouts}

## The sidebar

The sidebar shows your address at the top, the **Compose** button, the folders of the account, and the other screens of Mailwoman.

![The sidebar with markers on Compose, the Inbox and its counts, the user folders, the Attachments and Outbox entries, the app entries, and the Settings and Log out buttons.](/screenshots/user-mailbox/tour-sidebar.webp){.mw-crop}

1. **Compose** opens a new message in its own tab. See [Writing messages](/user/mailbox/composing).
2. The folders. The two numbers on a folder are its unread messages and its total messages, as in **4 / 15**. Inbox, Drafts, Sent, Archive, Spam and Trash come first.
3. **User folders** groups the folders you created, such as **Projects** and **Receipts**. See [Folders and labels](/user/mailbox/folders-labels).
4. **Attachments** lists every attachment in the account ([Attachments](/user/mailbox/attachments)). **Templates** ([Templates](/user/mailbox/templates)), **Events** and **Outbox** are in the same group.
5. **Apps**: Notifications, Calendar, Tasks, Notes, Contacts and Keys.
6. The **Settings** button, and next to it **Log out**.
{.mw-callouts}

The sidebar can be hidden or reduced to icons with the first two buttons of the toolbar, described next.

## The toolbar

The toolbar above the message list holds the buttons that change how the mailbox is laid out and what it shows.

![The workspace toolbar above the message list, with markers 1 to 10 on its buttons from Hide folders sidebar to Check for new mail.](/screenshots/user-mailbox/tour-toolbar.webp){.mw-crop}

1. **Hide folders sidebar**. When the sidebar is hidden the button is named **Show folders sidebar**.
2. **Compact folder icons** reduces the sidebar to icons, with only the unread count beside each folder.
3. **Select messages** turns on selection mode for the list. See [Selecting several messages](/user/mailbox/bulk-actions).
4. **Reading pane** chooses where the open message appears.
5. **Close message** closes the open message. It is on the toolbar only while a message is open.
6. **Show or hide message list**, also only while a message is open.
7. **View arrangement** chooses how the tabs share the screen.
8. **Order by** sorts the message list.
9. The undo and redo group: **Nothing to undo** and **Recent actions to undo**, then **Nothing to redo** and **Actions to redo**. The first button reverses your last change to messages, for example a label you added. The button beside it lists recent actions.
10. **Check for new mail**.
{.mw-callouts}

### Reading pane, View arrangement and Order by

![The Reading pane menu with the choices Right, Bottom and Off.](/screenshots/user-mailbox/menu-reading-pane.webp){.mw-crop}

**Reading pane** has three choices: **Right** (the default), **Bottom** and **Off**.

![The View arrangement menu with Tabs, Side by side, Stacked, Masonry side by side and Masonry stacked.](/screenshots/user-mailbox/menu-arrangement.webp){.mw-crop}

**View arrangement** has five choices: **Tabs** (the default), **Side by side**, **Stacked**, **Masonry side by side** and **Masonry stacked**. The side-by-side and stacked choices show two views next to each other or above and below each other, and you drag a tab onto either view to replace it.

![The Order by menu with twelve sort orders, then a Group by entry and the option Group messages into conversations.](/screenshots/user-mailbox/menu-order.webp){.mw-crop}

**Order by** offers **Newest first** (the default), **Oldest first**, **Sender**, **Sender, Z to A**, **Subject**, **Subject, Z to A**, **Sent, newest first**, **Sent, oldest first**, **Importance, high first**, **Importance, low first**, **Size, largest first** and **Size, smallest first**. Under **Group by**, the checkbox **Group messages into conversations** is off by default.

## The message list

Each message is a row with the sender, the date and time, the subject and the first words of the text.

![One row of the message list, Spring reading list, with its hover buttons Pin, Snooze, Label, Flag for follow-up, Archive and Delete.](/screenshots/user-mailbox/tour-row.webp){.mw-crop}

1. The buttons that appear on a row when you point at it: **Pin**, **Snooze**, **Label**, **Flag for follow-up**, **Archive** and **Delete**.
{.mw-callouts}

- A bold sender with a yellow mark at the left edge is an unread message.
- A paperclip beside the date means the message has an attachment.
- A label you applied shows as a coloured tag on the row ([Folders and labels](/user/mailbox/folders-labels)).
- In search results, each row also shows the folder the message is in ([Searching](/user/mailbox/search)).

Click a row to open the message ([Reading messages](/user/mailbox/reading)). Right-click a row for the full list of actions, including **Open in a new tab**.

## Tabs

The **Mail** tab is always there. Compose, Attachments, Templates, Settings and attachments you open from a message each get their own tab with a close button. Choosing **Open in new tab** on a folder, or **Open in a new tab** on a message, opens that folder or message in a tab too.
